Inbound Stack

A five-player baseline inbounds play. Form the stack, pick the break, then reset or switch.

STEP 1: STACK
STEP 2: BREAK (3 options)
STEP 3: RESET (2 options)
Solid arrow: player movement. Dashed arrow: pass. Ball is the orange dot. Player 1 always inbounds from behind the rim; Reset sends players 2 through 5 to new random spots inside the 3-point arc, and Switch moves the inbounder to the other side. With Cycle on, pick one Reset option, the Stack and one Break and the play loops until you turn Cycle off.
