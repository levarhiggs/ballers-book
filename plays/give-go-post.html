Give Go Post aka “GGP”

A five-player half-court set. Run the setup, then pick how the attack finishes.

STEP 1: SETUP (2 options)
STEP 2: ATTACK (3 options)
STEP 3: RESET
Solid arrow: player movement. Dashed arrow: pass. Ball is the orange dot. The Attack buttons apply to whichever Setup you just ran. With Cycle on, pick one Position, one Setup and one Attack button and the play loops until you turn Cycle off.
